## Interface Contracts & Personas
- **务实架构师 (Pragmatic Architect)**: Focuses on system stability, toolchain simplicity, maintenance cost, strict boundaries, and determinism.
- **激进AI信仰者 (Radical AI Believer)**: Pushes for autonomous agents, deep LLM graph rewriting, minimal human intervention, complex Agentic pipelines, and fully automated graph evolution.
- **人类体验官 (Human Experience Officer)**: Advocates for human cognitive control, UX clarity, review efficiency, non-intrusive automation, and preventing information overload or unverified agent noise.
